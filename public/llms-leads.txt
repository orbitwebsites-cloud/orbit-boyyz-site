## How the data is sourced

- We find the businesses in your niche on Google Maps and on their own websites.
- We identify the actual owner of each business, with their name and title.
- Every email is verified before it ships. An email counts as verified only when it comes back "valid".
- Catch-all and risky addresses are never sold as verified.
