## Offer

- 25 free leads in one niche and one area.
- Delivered as a sheet by email within a few business days.
- No call, no calendar and no card: request them with a short form.
- Example niches: roofers, HVAC, plumbers, landscapers, electricians, home cleaning, auto repair.
- Need the outreach run too? We can send it for you from your own domains.

## What is in every lead

- Company
- Owner name and title, where public sources show them
- Verified email
- Business phone
- Website
- City / State
- Google rating
- Google reviews

Delivered as a sheet, one row per lead.

## How the data is sourced

- We find the businesses in your niche and area on Google Maps, then read each business's own website.
- We add the owner's name and title where public sources show it.
- Every email is verified before it ships. An email counts as verified only when it comes back "valid".
- Catch-all and risky addresses are never marked as verified.
