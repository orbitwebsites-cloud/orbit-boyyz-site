# Design direction (2026-11-11 revamp)

Reading this as: a marketing agency site for New Jersey home-service owners and outreach teams, in a quiet editorial style with one loud idea per screen. Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.

The owner's brief: one small clear statement in the hero, clean and minimal, real scroll-driven motion, and the page exists to get people into `/book`.

## Why each choice

- **Ink, paper, one orange.** Two neutrals so sections can flip between light and dark as a scene change, and a single accent reserved for the thing that matters on each screen (the booked estimate, the price, the button). Orange because it reads as trade and job-site, not as software.
- **Geist for everything, Instrument Serif italic for the one emphasised phrase.** A plain grotesque keeps long copy easy for owners reading on a phone; the italic serif marks the outcome in each headline ("booked jobs", "booked estimates").
- **Small mono labels in sentence case.** They name the section the way a form field is labelled. No uppercase, no wide tracking.
- **The orbit.** The brand's own mark, used as the single motif: the hero planet that grows into the next section, the ring that closes on the closing call to action.
- **Scroll scenes instead of feature cards.** Each scene shows a mechanism rather than describing it: a lead going from ad tap to booked estimate, a wireframe becoming a site, a month of leads filling in. Motion is tied to scroll so the reader controls the pace.
- **Crossed-out metrics scene.** States the positioning (paid on bookings, not on clicks) in one image.
- **Cards only for choices.** The two offers and the pricing tiers are cards because the reader is choosing between them. Everything else is type on a surface.
- **Arrows only on the booking button and text links.** They mean "this goes somewhere". List items use a short rule instead.
- **One action.** Every button goes to `/book`; the phone number is the only alternative.

## Honesty rules carried over from the old site

- No testimonials, client counts or results that do not exist.
- The phone conversation and the "Dan's Kitchens" site are labelled as examples on screen.
- Lighthouse numbers are labelled as targets.

## Known gaps

- Inner pages (services, pricing, blog, trade and town pages) are dark-only. They use the new palette, type and buttons but not the light/dark scene changes.
- `/book` keeps its own lighter layout and stylesheet.
