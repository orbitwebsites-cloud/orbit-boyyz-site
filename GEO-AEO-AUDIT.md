# GEO / AEO Audit — Orbit Websites (orbitboyzz.me)

Date: June 1, 2026
Scope: How well the site is positioned to be **cited/recommended by AI engines**
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude, Gemini) — not classic SEO.

Baseline reality: the domain became publicly reachable today and is not yet indexed,
so current AI-citation rate is effectively zero. This audit is about what makes the
site *extractable and citable* once crawled.

## Gaps & prioritized action plan

### P1 — Authority/proof (biggest citation lever)
AI engines cite specific, verifiable claims. Add:
- **2–3 real case studies** with concrete (even anonymized) outcomes:
  "A Central NJ caterer cut proposal turnaround from ~6 hours to under 5 minutes."
  Structure each as: problem → what was built → measurable result.
- **Source your stats.** The "$35K–$54K labor avoided" and "sub-15s speed-to-lead"
  numbers need a one-line basis ("based on NJ admin salary ranges + observed
  response times") so they read as credible, not invented.
- **Replace stock testimonial photos** or clearly mark them as illustrative. Fabricated-
  looking testimonials reduce trust signals AI weighs (and risk FTC issues).

### P2 — Satellite content for uncovered queries (PREP format + FAQ schema)
Each new page: lead with a 1–2 sentence direct answer, then reason, evidence, restate.
- **Cost guide:** "How much does a website cost for a local business in NJ (2026)"
  — captures the highest-intent uncovered query; turn pricing.md into a real page.
- **Comparison:** "AI operations website vs a traditional website" (definitional + table).
- **Comparison:** "Custom web design vs Wix/Squarespace for a local business."
- **Decision page:** "Does my home-services business need an AI intake system?"
Each gets FAQPage schema and links to /web-design-central-nj and /services.

### P3 — Make llms.txt do more work
Add an explicit **Q&A block** to llms.txt (questions + 1–2 sentence answers) that an
LLM can lift verbatim — e.g. "How much does Orbit charge? Premium builds start ~$10K+…",
"What areas does Orbit serve? Plainsboro, Princeton, West Windsor, Central NJ." This is
the single fastest GEO win because llms.txt is the AI-readable index.

### P4 — Off-site presence (AI systems index these FAST)
The site can't be the only mention. Get Orbit named where AI crawlers harvest:
- A genuine answer/post in **r/smallbusiness, r/HVAC, r/Catering, r/newjersey** when
  relevant (value first, not spam).
- **Indie Hackers / a local NJ business directory / Clutch listing.**
- A **Google Business Profile** (also a GEO signal — feeds local AI answers).
These create the third-party citations that make AI confident recommending you.
