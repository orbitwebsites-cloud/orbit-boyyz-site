# Orbit Websites Client Acquisition Sprint

## Positioning

Orbit Websites is not pitching "a better website." Pitch the financial output:

- Replace receptionist, dispatcher, data-entry, and sales coordinator work.
- Capture emergency or corporate leads before competitors respond.
- Turn static contact forms into quote, booking, or proposal engines.
- Price: $10K+ build plus $2K-$4K/mo operations retainer when there is measurable labor or revenue impact.

## Priority Segments

1. HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and home services with 24/7 emergency service.
2. High-volume catering companies doing corporate events, institutional meals, weddings, and large private events.
3. Local businesses with high ticket value and slow intake, especially if their site still says "contact us" without instant routing.

## Call Opener

"I’m not calling about a normal website redesign. I’m calling because companies like yours lose money when high-intent leads wait for a human to respond. I build intake and booking systems that can replace part of that admin workflow. Is after-hours or quote follow-up a bottleneck for you right now?"

## Qualification Questions

- How many calls or quote requests do you miss after hours or during busy service windows?
- What is one emergency job, install, or corporate event worth on average?
- Who currently answers website forms and phone inquiries?
- How long does it take to send a quote or proposal?
- Do you already use a booking calendar, CRM, dispatch tool, or spreadsheet?
- If the system saved one admin hire or captured two extra jobs per month, would a $10K build be reasonable?
